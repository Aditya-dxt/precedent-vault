**Scoring Formula:**

```
Repeat Frequency  = (Matched Questions in PYQs) / (Total PYQ Questions) × adjustment
Marks Weight      = (Marks of Matched Questions) / (Total PYQ Marks)
Composite Score   = (Frequency × 0.60) + (Marks Weight × 0.40)
```
